04Analytical models

01

Cost-plus credit pricing

credits = ⌈ costusd ÷ ( valuecredit × (1 − m) ) ⌉ ,  m = 0.50

Every model, resolution and duration is priced from its measured provider cost, so margin holds when providers change prices. A turn is charged max(tier floor, real cost).

02

Weighted interview score

Sr = Σi=1..8 wi·xi ÷ 100 ,  Ŝ = mean(Sr) ,  flag ⇔ max Sr − min Sr ≥ 3.0

Weights favour the craft skills of the role (23 + 23 of 100). Bands: ≥ 8.5 strongly recommend, ≥ 7.0 recommend, ≥ 5.5 neutral.

03

Unit economics of an AI add-on

LTV = ARPU × gross margin × lifetime ,  cbreak-even = costfree users ÷ ( LTV × N )

The Career Agent business case: at a 54 % gross margin the add-on breaks even at ≈ 5.6 % conversion against a 4 % assumption, which is why conversation now runs on a ~50× cheaper model and only documents use the stronger one.

04

Content performance model

lift = median(views | collab) ÷ median(views | solo) ≈ 52×

109 Instagram posts classified by format, topic, era and partner from caption text, reproducible end to end. Collaborations outperformed solo posts by a median factor of about 52.

06Data quality & governance

01

Isolation by design

Two products share one database; separation lives in schema, storage prefix and a scoped client, verified by an automated isolation suite.

02

Least privilege

Row-level security on every table, public roles revoked by default, sensitive files in private buckets behind short-lived signed links.

03

Exactly-once money

Compare-and-swap status updates, unique keys per order and claim-before-send rows make webhooks, crons and retries safe to repeat.

04

Time is a data type

Business dates in the market’s time zone (WIB), periods closed at 23:59 local, never inferred from UTC.

05

Monitored quality

Daily health checks reconcile discounts, usage counters and orders; every outbound link in an AI answer is opened before it is kept.

06

Recoverable

Weekly numbered backups with per-table row counts and checksums, plus lossless storage compression verified pixel by pixel.
